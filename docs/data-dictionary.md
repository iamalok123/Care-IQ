# CareIQ Data Dictionary & Master Schema Reference

This dictionary documents all domain entities, required fields, types, allowed values, provenance levels, and privacy boundaries for the CareIQ platform.

---

## 1. Provenance & Metadata Standard

Every record in the CareIQ ecosystem tracks provenance to maintain transparency and user trust under stressful hospital decisions.

### 1.1 `data_status`
- `PUBLIC_REFERENCE`: Sourced from public government/hospital portals.
- `USER_PROVIDED`: Entered or confirmed explicitly by the caregiver or patient.
- `SYNTHETIC`: Deterministically generated mock data for demonstration.
- `DERIVED`: Computed deterministically by rules engines.
- `AI_GENERATED`: Generated by LLM text extraction or natural language engines.

### 1.2 `verification_status`
- `VERIFIED`: Confirmed by user or verified reference document.
- `PARTIALLY_VERIFIED`: Extracted or partially matched; requires user confirmation.
- `UNVERIFIED`: Raw or newly ingested data.
- `NEEDS_VERIFICATION`: Explicitly highlighted to caregiver to ask hospital/insurer.
- `EXPIRED`: Legacy or outdated policy data.

### 1.3 `confidence`
- `HIGH`
- `MEDIUM`
- `LOW`
- `UNKNOWN`

## 2. Core Entities

### 2.1 Users & Patients
- **`User`**: System account (`id`, `role`, `display_name`, `email`, `phone`, timestamps).
  - Roles: `PATIENT`, `CAREGIVER`, `DEMO_ADMIN`.
- **`Patient`**: Person receiving care (`id`, `user_id`, `display_name`, `age_band`, `gender`, `city`, `state`, `pincode`, `preferred_language`).
- **`CaregiverRelationship`**: Link between caregiver and patient (`id`, `caregiver_user_id`, `patient_id`, `relationship_type`, `permission_level`).
  - Permission levels: `VIEW`, `VIEW_AND_UPDATE`, `FULL_DEMO_ACCESS`.

### 2.2 Insurance & Policy Model
- **`Insurer`**: Insurance provider (`id`, `name`, `short_name`, `insurer_type`, `website`, provenance).
  - Types: `PRIVATE`, `PUBLIC`, `EMPLOYER`, `SCHEME_ADMINISTRATOR`.
- **`InsurancePolicy`**: Normalized policy record (`id`, `patient_id`, `insurer_id`, `policy_name`, `policy_type`, `sum_insured`, `remaining_sum_insured`, `room_eligibility`, `copay_percentage`, `deductible_amount`, `cashless_supported`, `preauthorization_supported`, `pre_hospitalization_days`, `post_hospitalization_days`, provenance).
  - Room Eligibility: `GENERAL`, `SEMI_PRIVATE`, `PRIVATE_AC`, `DELUXE`, `SUITE`, `ANY_ROOM`.
- **`PolicyRule`**: Specific policy constraints (`id`, `policy_id`, `rule_code`, `category`, `condition_json`, `result_json`, `priority`, provenance).
- **`PolicyExclusion`**: Non-covered treatments or items (`id`, `policy_id`, `category`, `description`, `normalized_code`, provenance).

### 2.3 Hospitals & Facilities
- **`Hospital`**: Healthcare provider (`id`, `name`, `hospital_type`, `ownership_type`, `address`, `city`, `district`, `state`, `pincode`, `latitude`, `longitude`, `beds`, `icu_beds`, `emergency_available`, `ambulance_available`, `open_24x7`, provenance).
  - Hospital Types: `MULTISPECIALTY`, `SPECIALTY`, `GENERAL`, `DIAGNOSTIC`, `PUBLIC`, `PRIVATE`, `TEACHING`.
- **`HospitalRoom`**: Room category availability and tariffs (`id`, `hospital_id`, `room_category_id`, `tariff_per_day`, `total_rooms`, `availability_status`, provenance).
- **`HospitalNetwork`**: Network mapping with insurers (`id`, `hospital_id`, `insurer_id`, `network_status`, `cashless_status`, `preauth_required`, provenance).
  - Statuses: `IN_NETWORK`, `OUT_OF_NETWORK`, `UNKNOWN`.

### 2.4 Procedures & Costs
- **`Procedure`**: Medical procedure or test (`id`, `code`, `name`, `category`, `description`, `decision_support_only`).
- **`ProcedureCost`**: Cost estimates for hospital & procedure (`id`, `hospital_id`, `procedure_id`, `min_cost`, `max_cost`, `typical_cost`, `currency`, provenance).
- **`CostComponent`**: Itemized breakdown (`id`, `procedure_cost_id`, `component_code`, `component_name`, `estimated_amount`, `coverage_candidate`, `data_status`).
  - Components: `ROOM`, `PROFESSIONAL_FEE`, `PROCEDURE`, `DIAGNOSTICS`, `MEDICINES`, `CONSUMABLES`, `OTHER`.

### 2.5 Care Journey & Verification
- **`CareJourney`**: Patient care trajectory (`id`, `patient_id`, `hospital_id`, `policy_id`, `scheme_id`, `current_stage`, `journey_status`, timestamps).
  - Stages: `ADMISSION`, `INVESTIGATION`, `PROCEDURE`, `RECOVERY`, `DISCHARGE`, `CLAIM_SUPPORT`, `COMPLETED`.
- **`JourneyEvent`**: Discrete event during care (`id`, `journey_id`, `stage`, `event_type`, `title`, `description`, `status`, `occurred_at`, `insurance_relevance`, `requires_verification`, `metadata_json`).
  - Statuses: `PENDING`, `CURRENT`, `COMPLETED`, `SKIPPED`, `BLOCKED`.
- **`VerificationItem`**: Action item for caregiver to check (`id`, `patient_id`, `journey_id`, `category`, `title`, `question`, `reason`, `priority`, `status`, timestamps).
  - Priorities: `LOW`, `MEDIUM`, `HIGH`.
- **`Recommendation`**: Decision-support recommendation (`id`, `patient_id`, `journey_id`, `recommendation_type`, `target_entity_type`, `target_entity_id`, `score`, `confidence`, `reason_json`, `explanation`, `requires_verification`).
